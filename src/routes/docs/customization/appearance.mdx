# Appearance Settings

Wino Mail offers various customization options to personalize your email experience through themes and visual styles.

## Theme Mode

Wino Mail supports both light and dark themes for comfortable viewing in different lighting conditions. You can:
- Switch between light and dark mode
- Have it follow your system preferences

## Application Themes

Choose from seven distinct visual themes to customize your email interface:

### Dynamic Themes
These themes adapt to your selected theme mode (light/dark):
- **Mica** - A modern, translucent design that matches your Windows theme
- **Acrylic** - Frosted glass effect with subtle transparency

### Static Themes
These themes maintain consistent appearance regardless of theme mode:
- **Nighty** - Dark and sophisticated
- **Forest** - Nature-inspired green tones
- **Clouds** - Clean and minimal bright design
- **Snowflake** - Cool, winter-inspired aesthetics
- **Garden** - Warm, natural color palette

<InfoBar severity="caution" closable>
  Only Mica and Acrylic themes support dynamic light/dark mode switching. Other themes maintain their unique appearance regardless of the theme mode setting.
</InfoBar>
